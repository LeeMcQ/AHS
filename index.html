The Complete BETTER LIVING Guide

B
Principle 1

Breathing Exercises

  1. Through breathing, the body gets its supply of oxygen which is so critical for body-cells to function. No oxygen, No life. Poor Oxygen, Poor Health.
  2. Good Breathing Exercises fill the lungs with more air / oxygen than normal, which increases oxygen levels in the blood and improves life.
  3. Do your proper breathing exercises every morning & every evening for three (3) minutes, preferably outside in the Open Air.
  4. Proper breathing means: use your diaphragm or abdomen. When you inhale your diaphragm should go down and your stomach should expand.
  5. Through proper breathing exercises your lungs can be filled with more air, which means healthier lungs, more oxygen in the blood, better cell function, less chance for illnesses, and new life to the soul.
  6. For proper breathing exercises: Stand with hands behind your head, inhale deep and breathe out slowly. Do it for 2–3 minutes twice a day.
  7. Good Advice: Always sit up straight; stand up straight; walk up straight.
Daily Practice

Breathe deeply every morning & evening for 3 minutes in the Open Air. Stand straight, hands behind your head — inhale deep, exhale slowly.

  5. Exercise boosts your energy level; Exercise can improve the libido.
  6. What more proof do you want — Exercise is Life-Saving to Your Body.

T
Principle 3

Temperance & Self-Control

Temperance or Self-control is to get rid of everything that is HARMFUL and to use wisely that which is HEALTHFUL to the body and soul. WATCH YOUR WEIGHT. We should also have self-control and discipline in our:

Words

Speech — the way we speak, words we use, speak at the right time

Actions

Behaviour — abstain from pre-marital sex, harmful substances, etc.

Emotions

Feelings — good sense of humour, be thankful, compassion

Thoughts

Pure, healthy, clean thoughts; Meditate on Christ; etc.

Things Harmful to the Body — LEARN TO SAY NO!

Alcohol, Drugs, Smoking, Premarital Sex, Violence, Stealing, Suicide, Anger, Depression, Wrong Eating & Drinking habits, Over-working, Harmful Viewing, etc.

"Lack of Self-control of our appetite will prove the ruin of thousands. If only we could have conquered on this point, we would have had moral power to gain the victory over every other temptation of Satan."

Life is full of challenges. Sometimes it seems overwhelming and we long for an escape. But choosing alcohol and drugs is no escape at all. Instead, alcohol and drugs become a prison of your own making, one that is very hard to be set free from.

Learn to Say NO! to DRUGS, say NO! to ALCOHOL, Say NO! to Addictive Behaviours, say YES! to LIFE.

David says in 1 Samuel 17:37 — "The Lord who delivered me from the paw of the lion and the paw of the bear will deliver me from the hand of the Philistine." The same God can deliver you from all your weaknesses, yes even substance abuse and your loss of self-control.

Principle 4

Thankful Heart

People at home, work or church are many times so unthankful. It is really rare to see an Attitude of Gratitude from spouses, family members, friends and colleagues today. The Bible emphasises the importance of a Thankful Heart.

Proverbs 17:22 — "A joyful heart is good medicine, but a broken spirit drains one's strength."

Many times, people find themselves in a state of Mental and Physical Exhaustion like Stress, Burnout, Anxiety, Depression and other Medical Illnesses because of a Negative Attitude. Such attitude brings many bad symptoms and illnesses on us.

Symptoms of a Tense Body
  • Your heart beats faster, you breathe faster
  • You feel an adrenaline rush, your muscles tighten up
  • Your pupils dilate
  • Illnesses: Hypertension, Diabetes, Sexual Dysfunction, high Cholesterol, Peptic Ulcers, Spastic Colon and many others become very common
Philippians 4:6
Don't worry about anything, but in all your prayers ask God for what you need, always asking him with a thankful heart.
1 Thessalonians 5:18
Be thankful in all circumstances. This is what God wants from you in your life in union with Christ Jesus.

Principle 5

Eat Healthy

No Junk Food. We recommend you eat a Big Breakfast, a Good Lunch and a Small Supper. With our low energy jobs of today (inactive), two (2) meals a day are many times sufficient for us.

AVOID or REDUCE DRASTICALLY
  1. Salt Intake (keep it as low as possible)
  2. Take-aways / Fast Foods (Burgers, Pizzas, Chips, Hotdogs, etc.)
  3. Organ Meat (Liver, Kidney, Tongue, Brain, etc.)
  4. Meat Intake (Beef, Lamb, Game, Sausage, etc.)
  5. Baked or Grilled food in Oil (Fried foods, Food baked in oil)
  6. Eggs (Not more than 3 per week or avoid)
  7. Cream & Ice-cream (Especially deserts that contain it)
  8. Poultry like Chicken, etc.
  9. Pork meat and processed meat
  10. Cheese
  11. Butter & Margarine
  12. Full cream Milk
  13. Chocolates
  14. Cakes & Biscuits, Cookies
  15. Alcohol & Smoking
USE MORE — Strongly Recommended
🌾 Bran / Fibre & Oats
🫘 Beans & Lentils
🌽 Maize & Rice (all forms)
🥦 Fruit & Vegetables
🫒 Olives & Olive Oil
🥑 Avocado

🌾 Whole Wheat & Grains
💧 2–2½L Pure Water / day

No Cooldrinks, Little Juice, Little Meat.

R
Principle 6

Rest Sufficiently

This is one of the principles in which many of us need some discipline. Be regular and consistent in your Rising and Retiring; Be on time with your Mealtimes; Make sure you have your daily Devotional time; Make sufficient time for Study; Have your daily Outdoor Exercises; and Rest Sufficiently.

All of the body needs rest. We spend one third of our time sleeping, usually 6–8 hours every day. Adequate sleep is essential to brain health; it also gives your brain the restorative breaks it needs to function at an optimal level. Adequate sleep also has a positive influence on the brain, the mood, your mental health, the pulse, blood sugar levels, blood pressure, body function, muscle tone, breathing and digestion.

🚫 Factors that PREVENT Good Sleep
  • Eating heavy foods late in the day
  • Coffee, tea, alcohol
  • Heavy exercise late in the day
  • Anger, worry, over-thinking, anxiety
  • Going to bed too late
  • Drugs
✅ Things that PROMOTE Good Sleep
  • A regular schedule
  • Breathing exercise, sunshine, physical exercise
  • Fresh air and good nutrition
  • Evening routine, a warm shower
  • A warm non-alcoholic, non-caffeine drink
  • A quiet, well-ventilated room
  • Reading God's Word and Praying before bed

Principle 7

Living Water

Key Fact: Our body consists of 70% water. If we don't maintain that we will feel tired, get dehydrated, drowsy, headaches, will have poor digestion. We will many times just feel sick and irritable.
Water assists with getting rid of toxins (poisons) from our bodies through kidneys and sweating
Water helps with normal functioning of the colon/bowels — keeps it clean
Water keeps the blood pure and helps with the transport for oxygen and nutrients to cells
Water cushions our body joints
Water helps regulate body temperature
Water helps the body not to form blood clots
Water helps with normal kidney function
Water lowers incidence of bladder infections and helps with chronic headaches
Daily Recommendation: Drink 2–3 litres of water per day (or 2 glasses for every 15kg body weight). Best water: Spring water / Mineral water / Reverse Osmosis / Filter water / Tap water. WATER IS FOR CLEANSING.
⚠️ Very Important: DON'T DRINK AND EAT

The water dilutes the digestive juices/acids that break down food. Digestion will take much longer. So, fluids only ½ hour before meals and 1 hour after meals please.

Common Illnesses from Poor Water Intake

Headaches, Constipation, Skin problems, Tiredness, Indigestion, Bladder infection; and the list can go on.

External Benefits of Water

Cleanliness, open pores on skin, water/hydro therapy (cold and hot) improves circulation and helps with colds, flu, arthritis, wounds, encourages healing, and many others.

⚠️ NEVER LEAVE PLASTIC WATER BOTTLES IN CAR OR IN SUN. Water in plastic bottles is not too safe especially when exposed to heat/sun. The chemicals in plastic can cause harm to our bodies.

Principle 10

In-the-SUN (Sunshine)

BENEFITS OF SUNLIGHT:
Elevates and lifts our spirits, helping us to overcome depression
Kills germs and keeps things fresh; deodorizes
Aids in keeping the skin healthy
Source of VITAMIN D (most Namibians have a Vit D Deficiency)
Regulates temperature, humidity & wind on earth
Stimulates the immune system
Aids in plant growth
Provides energy for the whole world
Lowers harmful LDL cholesterol
Lowers high blood pressure
Stabilizes blood sugar level

Your body produces vitamin D naturally when it's directly exposed to sunlight. Vitamin D helps to regulate the absorption of Calcium and Phosphate, it fights inflammation and disease, it regulates the mood, can help with weight-loss.

⚠️ Caution: Getting Too Much Sunlight is a Risk factor for skin cancer. Use a good sun screen with an SPF (sun-screen protection factor) of 8 or greater. Umbrellas, hats, sunglasses and long-sleeved clothing all help to protect the skin from over exposure.
How Much Sunlight Do You Need? One should get an average of 10–15 minutes of sunlight each day, during the hours before midmorning and after late afternoon. Darker skinned people need 30–60 minutes. Sunshine does not pass through glass. One must be in direct sunlight. Vit D Supplement is OK.

Principle 11

Nutritional Diet

The essential "big 4" nutrients (macronutrients) in our diet are Proteins, Carbohydrates, Fats and Liquids. And since there are both unhealthy and healthy eating choices for each of the four, here's how to choose wisely:

1. Healthy Protein

Comes from quality high protein foods. Good choices are Beans, Lentils, Peas, Low-fat yogurt, cheese and other low-fat dairy, eggs and nuts. Choose mainly lean chicken (without the skin) and omega 3 fish. Bake, broil or grill your poultry and fish to stay lean and healthy.

2. Healthy Fats

Have fat-soluble vitamins and essential fatty acids. Choose 100% whole grains, nuts, seeds and omega 3 fish. Use olive oil for salads and cooking. Keep saturated fats like meat and dairy low, your good fats at around 25% of calories and eliminate all trans-fats from diet.

3. Healthy Carbohydrates

Generally complex carbohydrates that have a low glycaemic index. Healthy carbs include fresh vegetables like raw carrots, red peppers, broccoli, kale, green leafy veggies and other high fibre foods like raw fruits, beans and healthy whole grains like brown rice, rolled oats, 100% whole wheat pasta, breads and crackers, plus low fat dairy. Avoid high glycaemic carbs.

4. Healthy Liquids

Water is an essential nutrient involved in every function of your body, including healthy digestion and elimination. Since water makes up two-thirds of your body, pure water is the best liquid to drink. Avoid sodas, fruit juice and other sweet drinks and limit alcohol and coffee.

A Few Good Tips for Healthy Eating

💧 Water / Fluids

2–2½ litres water per day. Purified or Boiled water is best. Chamomile tea, Green tea, Carrot juice, Tomato juice — excellent choices.

🌾 High Fibre

Vegetables & Salads (with skin), Fruit (with skin), Oats, Muesli, Legumes 2–4 times/week, Brown rice, Whole wheat pasta, Nuts & Seeds

⚠️ Reduce

Pizzas, Pastas, White bread, White rice, Sugar & Sugar products. Never use milk and sugar together.

🚫 Avoid

Coffee, Cola Drinks, Tea (only black rooibos tea allowed), Caffeine products. Organs (Liver, kidney, Brain, Tongue, etc.)

🍽️ Meals

Big Breakfast, Good Lunch. Skip supper if over 70 years or have something very light (Toast, fruit, etc.)

🏃 Exercise

Very, very important for the bowel system; it also releases STRESS on colon. Do 4 km of walking every day or 2nd day or any equivalent exercise.

Principle 12

God-Centred

We should make God part of every single plan in/of your life. A God-centred life means that EVERYTHING, yes, EVERYTHING belongs to the Lord. This includes our bodies, our minds, our tongues, our hands and feet, the money we possess, the vehicles we drive, the jobs we have, the clothes we wear, the friends we have, the time we have, the parents we have, the church we belong to, etc... EVERYTHING belongs to the Lord.

Therefore, we should treat everything, especially our bodies with the utmost respect and care — because we know it doesn't belong to us. It belongs to the Lord! (1Cor 3:16,17)

1 Corinthians 6:19,20
"You are bought with a price. So, use your bodies for God's glory."
Isaiah 43:1
"I have called you by name — you are mine, says the Lord."

We will realize that the common question that arises many times is where do I start to live a "Christ-centred life." It should start with me — reading my Bible, saying the right words at the right time, going to church, singing the right songs and praying the right prayers. When I start with Jesus, I can let the knowledge of who He is and what He has done, work its way to me. Don't get me wrong, being a Christian is a life marked by hard and dedicated work, and struggling. But before, within, and after all the work and struggle, we rest in God. We are comforted as we mourn. We are joyful in our sorrow. Why? Because the blood Jesus bled was bled on our behalf, the life Jesus lived was lived in our place, the death Jesus died was for our sin, and the power of God that raised Christ from the dead will resurrect all who believe. (see 1 Cor 5:21, Col 1:27)

When we borrow a friend's car — maybe it's a really nice car — we treat it with great respect because we don't want something bad to happen to it while it is in our possession. Well, this same principle applies to the things of the Lord. We are stewards and managers of these things, including our Health. While He gave Life to us, let us give our Good Health back to Him.

Pray About:
  • Wisdom and strength to follow a Healthy Lifestyle
  • Victory over addictions, weaknesses and battles in your life
  • A life partner. Ask God for direction and His leading.
  • A home you are considering to buy or rent. God's perfect will.
  • A job.
  • Any major financial crisis or financial decision you are about to make.
